# Bat-Signal

A corner companion that watches the user's Claude Code sessions and signals when one needs them. This glossary fixes the words the product, the code and the docs use for its ideas.

## Watching

**Case**:
One Claude Code session, as Bat-Signal shows it.
_Avoid_: Session (in the UI), job, mission

**Needs you**:
The list of things waiting on the user: a permission, an error, a question, a new reply, a stalled case.
_Avoid_: Alerts, inbox, attention list

**Stamp**:
The short, ink-stamped label that says why something is in Needs you or what state a case is in.
_Avoid_: Badge, tag, pill

**Signal**:
The disc parked in a screen corner that lights up when something needs the user; Bat-Signal's resting form.
_Avoid_: Widget, bubble, orb

## Look

**Theme**:
One version of Batman, from a film or a comic, and everything Bat-Signal wears to match it: palette, type, Bat-Clawd's costume, the emblem, the Signal, the Atmosphere and the Voice. The Batman (2022) is the default Theme.
_Avoid_: Skin, style, palette, "the night report theme"

**Layout**:
How the panel arranges the same content, independent of the Theme: the case files or the night report.
_Avoid_: Theme, view, mode

**Alarm color**:
The one color a Theme keeps for "something needs you"; it appears nowhere else. It is the hottest color in the Theme's palette, and a red that suits the palette when the palette has no color that reads as an alarm.
_Avoid_: Accent, brand color, "red"

**Accent**:
A Theme's color for emphasis that is not an alarm: the wordmark, focus, selection, switches, hover, the tab underline and progress. It never says that something needs the user.
_Avoid_: Alarm color, highlight, primary

**Voice**:
The tone of a Theme's flavor text: empty states, headings, the night report. It never renames a term.
_Avoid_: Copy, tone, strings

**Lexicon**:
A Theme's own names for Case, Needs you, the words inside a Stamp and the other terms above, shown in the panel and on the Signal only. It is on by default; off, every Theme uses the standard words. Windows notifications and the tray always use the standard words.
_Avoid_: Translation, aliases, slang

**Atmosphere**:
A Theme's ambient effect behind the panel, such as rain, snow or ash.
_Avoid_: Weather, particles, rain (as the general name)

**Bat-Clawd**:
Claude Code's pixel Clawd dressed as the Theme's Batman: a suit over his body, the cowl down to his eyes, the version's bat symbol on his chest and its cape or coat; Bat-Signal's mascot. Every Theme keeps Clawd's shape, his white eyes, his orange jaw and his moods.
_Avoid_: Mascot (alone), Clawd (alone), Batman

**Aura**:
The glow in the Theme's Alarm color around Bat-Clawd, shown only while something needs the user or in an iconic moment.
_Avoid_: Glow, halo, outline

**Mood**:
What Bat-Clawd is doing because of the cases: sleeping, flying or alarmed. Every Theme has the same moods.
_Avoid_: State, animation, pose

**Pose**:
How Bat-Clawd is drawn in a Mood or a moment. A Theme may swap any Pose for an iconic one from its version of Batman, as a homage.
_Avoid_: Sprite, frame, mood

**Signature pose**:
A Theme's most iconic Pose, shown on the perch, in the intro and when the user clicks Bat-Clawd.
_Avoid_: Easter egg, special mood

**Wave**:
A group of Themes built and released together.
_Avoid_: Batch, pack, season
